# Indian Capital Gains Taxation & Schedule CG Guide
**Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 (Budget 2024, effective July 23, 2024), Section 55(2)(ac) Grandfathering, Section 50AA & ITR Filing Reference**

---

## Executive Summary & Statutory Landmarks

The **Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 (effective July 23, 2024)** simplified the capital gains tax architecture in India by:
1. **Rationalizing holding periods** into two simple buckets: **12 months** (for listed securities, units of listed business trusts, and zero-coupon bonds) and **24 months** (for all unlisted financial and physical assets).
2. **Eliminating indexation** benefits across almost all financial assets (debt funds, unlisted equity, bonds).
3. **Unifying Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) rates** to **12.5%**.
4. **Raising Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) on listed equity / equity mutual funds** under Section 111A from **15% to 20%**.
5. **Expanding Section 112A annual exemption threshold** from **₹1,00,000 to ₹1,25,000**.
6. **Expanding Section 50AA deemed short-term classification** to include unlisted bonds and unlisted debentures transferred on or after July 23, 2024, in addition to specified mutual funds (<=35% equity) acquired on or after April 1, 2023.

```
       Prior to July 23, 2024                          On or After July 23, 2024
┌───────────────────────────────────────┐       ┌───────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Listed Equity: STCG 15%, LTCG 10%     │       │ Listed Equity: STCG 20%, LTCG 12.5%   │
│ Section 112A Exemption: ₹1,00,000     │  ───► │ Section 112A Exemption: ₹1,25,000     │
│ Holding Periods: 12, 24, or 36 months │       │ Holding Periods: 12 or 24 months      │
│ Indexation: Available under Sec 48    │       │ Indexation: Abolished for fin. assets │
│ Unlisted Debentures: Normal asset     │       │ Unlisted Debentures: Sec 50AA Deemed  │
└───────────────────────────────────────┘       └───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 1. Asset Classification & Holding Period Rules

Effective July 23, 2024, holding period thresholds for determining whether an asset is a **Short-Term Capital Asset (STCA)** or **Long-Term Capital Asset (LTCA)** are as follows:

| Asset Category | Pre-July 23, 2024 Holding Period | Post-July 23, 2024 Holding Period | Relevant Section / Proviso |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Listed Equity Shares** (on recognized Indian stock exchanges) | > 12 Months | > 12 Months | Section 2(42A), 1st Proviso |
| **Equity-Oriented Mutual Funds** (>65% domestic equity) | > 12 Months | > 12 Months | Section 2(42A), 1st Proviso |
| **Units of Business Trusts (REITs & InvITs)** | > 36 Months | **> 12 Months** *(Rationalized)* | Section 2(42A), 1st Proviso |
| **Zero Coupon Bonds (Listed/Approved)** | > 12 Months | > 12 Months | Section 2(42A), 1st Proviso |
| **Unlisted Equity Shares** | > 24 Months | > 24 Months | Section 2(42A), 2nd Proviso |
| **Bonds / Debentures (Listed)** | > 12 Months | > 12 Months | Section 2(42A) |
| **Specified Debt Mutual Funds** (<=35% equity, acquired >= 01-Apr-2023) | Always Short-Term | Always Short-Term | **Section 50AA** (Deeming fiction) |
| **Unlisted Bonds & Unlisted Debentures** (transferred >= 23-Jul-2024) | > 36 Months | **Always Short-Term** | **Section 50AA** (FA 2024 Amendment) |
| **Other Mutual Funds** (35%-65% equity, conservative hybrid, gold ETFs, pre-Apr 2023 debt) | > 36 Months | **> 24 Months** *(Reduced)* | Section 2(42A) |
| **Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs) - Secondary Market** | > 36 Months (unlisted) / > 12 Months (listed) | **> 12 Months** (listed) / > 24 Months (unlisted) | Section 2(42A) |
| **Unlisted Securities (other than shares/debentures)** | > 36 Months | **> 24 Months** | Section 2(42A) |

## 2. Tax Rates: Pre vs. Post July 23, 2024

### 2.1 Equity Shares (Listed) & Equity-Oriented Mutual Funds (STT Paid)
- **Short-Term Capital Gains (Section 111A)**:
  - Disposals up to July 22, 2024: **15%** (+ surcharge & 4% cess).
  - Disposals on or after July 23, 2024: **20%** (+ surcharge & 4% cess).
- **Long-Term Capital Gains (Section 112A)**:
  - Disposals up to July 22, 2024: **10%** on aggregate gains exceeding **₹1,00,000**.
  - Disposals on or after July 23, 2024: **12.5%** on aggregate gains exceeding **₹1,25,000**.
  - *No indexation benefit allowed under Section 112A.*

### 2.2 Unlisted Equity Shares
- **STCG**: Taxed at applicable individual income tax slab rates (Section 2(42A)).
- **LTCG (Section 112)**:
  - Disposals up to July 22, 2024: **20% with indexation** (residents) or **10% without indexation/FX fluctuation** (non-residents under Sec 112(1)(c)).
  - Disposals on or after July 23, 2024: **12.5% without indexation** for all investors.

### 2.3 Grandfathering under Section 55(2)(ac) (Pre-January 31, 2018 Holdings)
For listed equity shares or equity-oriented mutual fund units acquired on or before **January 31, 2018**, the cost of acquisition is computed using the grandfathering formula:

$$\text{Deemed Cost of Acquisition} = \max\Big(\text{Actual Cost}, \min(\text{FMV as of 31-Jan-2018}, \text{Full Value of Consideration / Sale Price})\Big)$$

**Key Rules & Nuances**:
- **Fair Market Value (FMV) on 31-Jan-2018**:
  - Traded on 31-Jan-2018: Highest quoted price on recognized stock exchange.
  - Not traded on 31-Jan-2018: Highest price on the immediately preceding trading day.
  - Mutual Fund unit: Net Asset Value (NAV) as of January 31, 2018.
- **Bonus Shares Issued Pre-31-Jan-2018**: Actual cost is ₹0. Deemed cost becomes $\min(\text{FMV on 31-Jan-2018}, \text{Sale Price})$.
- **Rights Issues Pre-31-Jan-2018**: Actual cost is the subscription price paid. Deemed cost formula applies identically.
- **Unlisted-to-Listed Conversions**: If acquired unlisted pre-Jan 31, 2018 and listed later, the indexed cost of acquisition up to 31-Jan-2018 serves as deemed FMV.
- **CBDT Scenarios**:
  - **Actual Cost < FMV < Sale**: Deemed cost = FMV (Gains up to 31-Jan-2018 fully exempt).
  - **Actual Cost < Sale < FMV**: Deemed cost = Sale (No capital gain and no artificial loss).
  - **Sale < Actual Cost < FMV**: Deemed cost = Actual Cost (Actual loss recognized; FMV cannot inflate loss).
  - **FMV < Actual Cost < Sale**: Deemed cost = Actual Cost (Gains above actual purchase price taxed).

## 3. Mutual Funds & Hybrid Classifications

1. **Equity-Oriented Mutual Funds (>65% domestic equity)**:
   - Holding Period: 12 months.
   - STCG: 20% (Sec 111A).
   - LTCG: 12.5% beyond ₹1.25 lakh (Sec 112A).
2. **Specified Mutual Funds under Section 50AA (<=35% domestic equity)**:
   - Acquired on or after April 1, 2023: Deemed short-term capital assets.
   - Taxed at the investor's **applicable slab rates** regardless of holding length.
   - No LTCG status, no indexation, no special concessional rates.
3. **Other Mutual Funds (35% to 65% domestic equity / Gold ETFs / Multi-Asset / Pre-April 2023 Debt Funds)**:
   - Holding Period: Reduced from 36 months to **24 months** effective July 23, 2024.
   - STCG (<= 24 months): Taxed at applicable slab rate.
   - LTCG (> 24 months):
     - Transferred on or after July 23, 2024: **12.5% without indexation**.
     - Transferred prior to July 23, 2024: **20% with indexation**.
   - *Grandfathering for Pre-April 1, 2023 Debt Funds*: Retain LTCG classification if held >24 months (post Budget 2024), taxed at 12.5% without indexation (Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 removed indexation for debt funds as well).

## 4. Bonds & Debentures

1. **Listed Bonds / Debentures**:
   - Holding Period: 12 months.
   - STCG: Slab rates.
   - LTCG: **12.5% without indexation** under Section 112 (proviso to Sec 48 has always denied indexation to bonds/debentures).
2. **Unlisted Bonds / Debentures (Section 50AA Amendment)**:
   - Under Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024, **all unlisted bonds and unlisted debentures transferred, redeemed, or maturing on or after July 23, 2024** are deemed **short-term capital assets** under Section 50AA.
   - Taxable at **slab rates** regardless of holding period.

---

## 5. Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs)

1. **Redemption with RBI**:
   - At maturity (8 years) or early redemption windows (after 5th year): **100% Tax-Exempt** for individual investors under **Section 47(viic)**.
2. **Secondary Market Sale (Stock Exchange)**:
   - Not exempt under 47(viic).
   - Holding period: 12 months (listed).
   - STCG: Slab rates; LTCG: **12.5%** post July 23, 2024.
3. **Semi-Annual Interest (2.5% p.a.)**:
   - Taxable under "Income from Other Sources" at slab rate. No TDS.

---

## 6. REITs & InvITs (Business Trusts)

1. **Listed Units**:
   - Holding period: **12 months** (reduced from 36 months effective July 23, 2024).
   - STCG: **20%** (Section 111A).
   - LTCG: **12.5%** (Section 112A / Section 112).
2. **Distributions**:
   - Interest: Taxable at slab rate.
   - Dividend: Exempt if SPV is under old corporate tax regime; taxable at slab rate if under Section 115BAA.
   - Capital reduction / Repayment of debt: Taxable under Section 56(2)(xii) if exceeding issue price reduction.

---

## 7. Dividends & TDS

- Dividends from Indian companies and mutual funds are taxable at individual slab rates under "Income from Other Sources".
- TDS under Section 194 (Equity shares) & Section 194K (Mutual funds): **10%** if aggregate dividend paid to a resident exceeds ₹5,000 in a financial year.

## 8. Set-Off, Carry-Forward & Schedule CG Mapping

### 8.1 Intra-Head & Inter-Head Set-Off Rules (Sections 70 & 71)
1. **Short-Term Capital Loss (STCL)**:
   - Can be set off against both STCG and LTCG.
2. **Long-Term Capital Loss (LTCL)**:
   - Can ONLY be set off against LTCG. Cannot be set off against STCG.
3. **Inter-Head Restriction**:
   - Capital losses (STCL or LTCL) cannot be set off against any other head of income (e.g. Salary, House Property, Business, Other Sources).

### 8.2 Carry-Forward of Losses (Section 74)
- Unabsorbed STCL and LTCL can be carried forward for up to **8 Assessment Years** immediately succeeding the assessment year in which the loss was incurred.
- Pre-condition: Tax return must be filed on or before the due date under **Section 139(1)**.
- Carried-forward STCL can offset future STCG or LTCG.
- Carried-forward LTCL can ONLY offset future LTCG.
